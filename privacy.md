# Privacy Policy for EzMemory

Effective date: 2026

EzMemory respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

EzMemory does not collect personal information.

All flashcards, decks, and study data are stored locally on your device.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your study data may be stored in your personal iCloud account and synchronized across your devices using Apple's iCloud services.

This data is managed by Apple and is not accessible to the developer.

## Third-Party Content

EzMemory allows users to import flashcard decks that may contain text, images, or audio created by third parties.

The developer of EzMemory does not provide, host, or distribute this content.

Users are responsible for the content they import.

## Analytics

EzMemory does not use analytics or tracking technologies.

## Contact

If you have questions, feedback, or bug reports, please open an issue on GitHub:
